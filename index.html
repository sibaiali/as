02 — Nutrition & Recovery

Fuel the Machine
🔥 2,500–2,700 kcal / day
🥩 150–170g Protein / day
🌾 2–3g/kg Carbs (timed)
💧 3–4L Water / day
🟢
On Track
Hit 150–170g protein daily. Prioritize: chicken, fish, lean beef, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey. Carbs around workouts — rice, potatoes, fruit. Healthy fats (olive oil, avocado, nuts) for hormones.
⚠️
Caution — If Abs Aren't Showing by Mid-Cycle
Drop calories by 200 kcal/day. Monitor weight weekly. Target ~0.5–1% bodyweight loss per week (0.5–0.85kg). You can't spot-reduce fat — diet is what reveals the abs you've built.
🔴
Reminder — Sleep & Deload
7–9 hours/night. Muscle is built during recovery, not training. If joints feel beat up by week 8–10, take a deload week (50% volume) before pushing to peak. Never skip rest days before heavy sessions.

04 — Exercise Library

Every Movement — With Form Cues
⏱️
Golden Rule — Tempo on Every Rep
2–4s down · pause at bottom · explode up (3-1-1 tempo). This maximises time-under-tension (~40–70s per set) — the #1 driver of hypertrophy without weights. If 15 reps feel easy, slow down before adding variation.
🎥 Demo
Mon / Wed / Fri · Shoulders & Upper Chest
Pike Push-Ups
💪 4 × 8–12 · Rest 60–90s
Feet elevated, hips high in a V-position. Descend with head just touching floor. Core tight, back straight. Increase difficulty by elevating feet higher or slowing the eccentric to 4s. Primarily hits anterior deltoids and upper chest.
🎥 Demo
Mon / Wed / Fri · Chest (Deep Stretch)
Deficit Push-Ups
💪 4 × 10–15 · Rest 60–90s
Hands on two sturdy books or chairs — dip lower than floor level for a deep chest stretch. This mechanical drop overloads chest fibers through a full range. Too easy? Elevate feet or add a 2s pause at the bottom.
🎥 Demo
Mon / Wed / Fri · Upper Chest & Front Delts
Decline Push-Ups
💪 3 × 10–15 · Rest 60–90s
Feet elevated on a chair or sofa, hands on floor. Body angles downward. Chest should nearly touch the floor at the bottom — full range of motion is non-negotiable. Increase chair height each week for progressive overload.
🎥 Demo
Mon / Wed / Fri · Chest & Anterior Deltoid
Pseudo-Planche Push-Ups
💪 3 × 8–12 (to failure on last set)
Hands by hips instead of shoulders, lean body forward before lowering. This shifts load dramatically onto chest and front delts — much harder than it looks. Go to failure on the last set only. Rest 90s minimum.
🎥 Demo
Wed · Chest & Front Shoulders
Chest Dips / Bench Dips
💪 3 × 8–15 · Rest 60–90s
Use two sturdy chairs for parallel dips, or one chair for bench dips. Lean torso forward to load the chest more than the triceps. Descend until shoulders are below elbows. Control the negative — 3s down.
🎥 Demo
Mon / Wed / Fri · Core — Abs & Obliques
Hollow Body Hold + V-Ups + Leg Raises
💪 Hollow: 3×45–60s · V-Ups: 4×15 · Leg Raises: 3×15
Hollow Hold: Press lower back into floor, lift shoulders & legs ~6 inches. Breathe controlled — don't let lower back arch.

V-Ups: Arms overhead → simultaneously lift legs and torso, touch toes. Control the descent slowly.

Leg Raises: Slow and controlled, progressive overload over weeks.

05 — Before Every Session

Warm-Up Protocol (10 min)
🔄
Joint Circles
1–2 min gentle shoulder and wrist circles. Prime connective tissue before any pressing.
🦴
Scapular Push-Ups
1×15 — lock out arms, squeeze and protract shoulder blades. Activates stabilisers.
🐱
Cat-Camel / Thoracic Extension
1–2 min. Mobilise the spine and thorax — critical for overhead pressing patterns.
💨
Wall Slides / Arm Crosses
1×10 each. Open the chest, warm the front deltoids, groove shoulder external rotation.
🏃
Dynamic Chest Stretch
Arm swings and trunk twists. Finish with 5 slow push-ups to groove form before the first set.
